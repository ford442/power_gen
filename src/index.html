Bench field B

Source: local bench slider
Educational Lorentz-force model (F = I ℓ × B on a sliding armature, with friction and back-EMF) — a low-voltage bench rail motor, not a railgun design tool. B is a local bench parameter by default; with Field coupling on it follows the MHD channel's simulated B estimate instead — still simulated, not metrology.

Jumping ring

The drive slider sets the AC supply; the ring's hover height follows it. The ring is a shorted single turn — its induced current opposes the primary (Lenz), the two repel, and the coupling k(h) decays as the ring rises, so the lift decays with it and the ring settles instead of leaving the pole.
Educational Thomson-ring demo (lumped circuit + rigid body) — not an induction-furnace or launcher model, and there is no projectile. There is also no thermal model at all: a real shorted ring heats and can glow or melt, and nothing here represents that.

Lab field coupling

Off by default — each bench keeps its own local B. On, the destination B is the source device's simulated estimate, clamped to the destination's catalog range. Not Maxwell, not metrology.
